Psychological help at Nazarbayev University

Mental Health Hotline · On-Duty Consultant (Triage)

The first point of contact. After an initial assessment, the consultant helps find the right level of support and, if needed, refers you to a psychologist, psychiatrist or other specialist.

🕗 Mon–Fri, 08:30–17:30

Can’t attend? Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours in advance via email or the on-duty counselor on Telegram.

🚨 Crisis support

In acute distress or if safety is a concern: during PCS hours contact the Psychological Counseling Service for urgent help. If there is an immediate threat to life, call emergency services (112) right away. Outside working hours, use the Emergency & Out-of-Hours contacts on the NU website.

🔒 Confidential

Contacts with PCS are confidential. Students and employees get professional support in a safe, respectful environment.
